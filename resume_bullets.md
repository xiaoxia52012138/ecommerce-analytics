# Resume Bullets — E-Commerce Analytics Project

Ready-to-paste, quantified, domain-agnostic bullets:

- Built an end-to-end analytics pipeline in Python (pandas) on **777K e-commerce
  transactions**, cleaning duplicates, cancelled orders, and invalid records to
  produce a reliable **£17.1M** revenue dataset spanning 41 countries.
- Performed **RFM customer segmentation** with quantile scoring, identifying
  that the top 3 segments drive **89.9% of revenue**; flagged 1,290 at-risk /
  hibernating customers as re-engagement targets.
- Quantified customer loyalty and basket behavior: **72.3% repurchase rate**,
  **£466 average order value**, and ~287 items per order, revealing a
  wholesale-like purchasing pattern.
- Derived product categories via keyword mapping on 4,000+ SKUs (76% revenue
  coverage) and ranked performance — Kitchen & Dining led at **£2.5M (14.8%)**;
  showed the UK concentrates **83.7%** of revenue, highlighting international
  expansion as a growth lever.
